I recently turned 26, and I want to live this year more intentionally than ever
before. 25 was packed with challenging moments that gave rise to several
oppoprtunities for personal growth. Here is what I want to work on.

{{<sizeable_img src="IMG_1249.jpg" process_cmd="resize x550" caption="Winding Roads Ahead">}}

## Lower my Cortisol

Having Shingles was a wake-up call that I'm too stressed out. Getting Shingles
three days before IM Switzerland quite literally showed me how stress gets in
between me and my goals.

1. Less worrying about the future. I fear uncertainty and risk without
   recognizing that, if carefully managed, can lead to far greater rewards over
   the long term.
2. Less worrying about my point-in-time performance, instead focus on the
   slope. Dial in the process of improving and get good at doing the work
   instead of just thinking about it.  
3. Get better at regulating emotions and learn to convert unpleasant
   emotions into positive outcomes. E.g., jealousy can be reframed as
   admiration. Be humble and learn from others.
4. Don't take failure too seriously. It's usually not that deep bruh.

## Resist the Slop

Thinking is now abundant and cheap, and life's incentives will always push you
to delegate as much of it away as possible. Resist the urge to protect your
humanity.

1. Force myself to practice original thinking. Resist slop, never outsource
   critical thinking. 
2. Less scrolling, more focus dediciated towards real life.
3. Related: prevent fear and anxiety distracting me from flow state. Enter flow
   state more frequently, i.e. at least once a day.

## Find Courage 

Fortune favors the courageous, and sometimes you just have to go for it.
Concretely:

1. Pursue work that actually excites me. 
2. Be willing to work hard towards your goals, even if the rewards are
   uncertain.
3. Never be afraid to take the first step. You can learn as you go. 
4. Build in public. Doesn't matter if it is unfinished or imperfect. Visible
   progress is often just as powerful as the finished result.
