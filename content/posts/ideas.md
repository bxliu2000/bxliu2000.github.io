Clicks are more efficient than typing words to perform actions on ui.

If you frame every user-interaction as: explore -> exploit, then words are much
better for the explore portion.

If you're given a page, say JIRA. Then you ask "How do I create a task?" The AI can give a step-by-step guide
of the site that performs user actions.

Generally, I've found AI useful for pointing me in the direction of information I didn't previously know,
and I can explore the rest myself.

For example, at Two Sigma I want the gpt to replace all the #ask or #help channels. Most of my questions are
fairly basic.

Or, at least when I'm learning a new thing, I can have it list general topics or existing ideas and then I can
do further research on these topics myself.
