# What is this post about?

Originally, I wanted to write about how your mind guides your fate in life. Overtime, your life becomes an embodiment of your thoughts. Whatever you believe you be true will eventually become true.

# Who is the audience?

The concept of "ego" is western. Your consciousness lives in a stream of thoughts, or a "living room" of thoughts. When you meditate, it becomes slightly more apparent that thoughts just "appear" in your head.

There is kind of like a closed feedback loop going on, where your consciousness informs the unconscious, and then vice versa. There is a theory that the consciousness lives in the "communication layer", which could help explain why Language has been known to direct thought.

In this sense, thinking becomes equivalent to talking to yourself. Perception is also highly biased toward objectives. You tend to find what you are looking for.

You will become what you think about.

Your idea of yourself is just the conscious mind making sense of the collections of inputs that it feels.

Your idea of self, the ego, is not necessarily reliable, and also not part of the "internal" brain, which is why it is impossible to understand it.

1. Your internal mental dialogue dictates the kind of person you will become.
2. You live in the stories that your mind tells itself.
   1. You cannot observe the objective "truth". The nature of the instrument you use to perceive the world necessarily applies a bias to your perspective. The best we can do is observe.
   2. Your inherent bias is some function which imitates your past experience (priors) and new experiences.
   3. Self-fulfilling prophecies.
3. Your mind has a super-power: free will. This can be a forcing function that takes you out of the current dip you are currently living in.
   1. You reap the seeds you sow. Seek and you shall find.
