# Basically

consistency, consistency, consistency. This is the only thing that will bring steady change over the long term. This is the mantra behind my physical fitness philosophy. Something > nothing. Take seasons seriously. The most important thing is to sustain an interest in, and a habit for doing endurance sports for the next 20 years. Choose your short term goals wisely, do not overcommit and do not burn out. It really is a numbers game.
